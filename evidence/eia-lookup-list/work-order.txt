PART B. The lookup list (data/eia/lookup-list.json)
1. One row per supported concept: product, activity, geography kind (state, country, united_states, eia_region), optional qualifier, sector mapping (form sector value -> EIA facet value or series code; blank = all sectors; or sector_supported: false), EIA route, fixed facet filters, data column, available frequencies, unit, measurement basis label, a one-line plain-English description for the screen ("Marketed production: all natural gas sold, before liquids such as propane are removed"), whether other bases exist, and the EIA metadata used to verify it. The geography is filled at lookup time from the form's code through the route's geography facet. Record that mapping per route.
2. Scope: REQUIRED = rows (or not_available entries) for every combination the 36 questions need. OPTIONAL = at most 40 more rows, in this priority: state and country electricity, natural gas, petroleum, coal, total energy. Where an EIA route is already organized by product and activity codes (e.g. international productId/activityId), map those codes once instead of row by row; every (productId, activityId) combination a mapping makes reachable counts as a row for C.3 (one sample country each), and combinations not checked are not used on the main path. Do not write not_available entries for combinations nobody asked for.
3. Several rows per concept are allowed when frequency differs (e.g. annual crude production from SEDS, monthly from petroleum/crd/crpdn). Choose by requested frequency. Without a match, use a labelled frequency fallback (existing behavior).
4. Units (settled, do not stop to ask): EIA's own published unit for that dataset, no conversion. Where EIA offers several, prefer U.S. physical units (short tons, cubic feet, barrels, kilowatt-hours/megawatt-hours, megawatts); total energy in Btu. State the unit on screen.
5. Truth comes from EIA metadata (route descriptions, facet values, series names) and EIA documentation, never titles alone. The cache may be used to find candidate codes; every row is confirmed live in C.3.
