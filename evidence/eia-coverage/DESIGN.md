## Established methods and benchmark applicability (replacement working agreement)

Checked 2026-10-29 before further behaviour or test design. The owner's replacement working agreement requires finding established methods and their benchmarks before inventing a method. It does not change Job 5's locked vocabulary, frozen records, pass marks, cost limits or implementation checkpoint.

- **Statistical correspondence tables:** [UNSD best-practice working-group draft, 2011, correspondence section](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/ExpertGroup/EGM2011/AC234-6.PDF) describes one-to-many, many-to-one and many-to-many links. This supports preserving alternative EIA bases and explicitly recording partial correspondence. It is methodology guidance, not an EIA mapping benchmark or certification of our classifications.
- **Equivalence versus narrower concepts:** [W3C SKOS Recommendation, section 10](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/#mapping) distinguishes exact, close, broader/narrower and related mappings. Use that distinction when reviewing whole-product versus subset correspondence; a related label is insufficient for equivalence. This is a semantic design basis, not a proposal to add RDF, an ontology engine or a dependency. Owner-approved measurement bases still require their EIA labels and units.
- **Energy definitions and authoritative identifiers:** [UN IRES, 2018, chapter III](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/energystats/methodology/documents/IRES-web.pdf) supplies the established SIEC hierarchy; [UNSD SIEC overview and revision material](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/Classifications/siec/) supplies current revision context. These aid review; they do not replace the owner's 14 products or EIA definitions. [EIA SEDS documentation guide, pp. 7–9](https://www.eia.gov/state/seds/sep_fuel/notes/use_guide.pdf) defines the source/product, activity and unit positions of five-character MSNs and links the official descriptions. Use full definitions to validate code-family correspondence; do not transfer a SEDS code interpretation to another dataset without checking it.
- **Mapping evaluation:** [OAEI methodology, 2005, section 3.1](https://oaei.ontologymatching.org/doc/oaei-methods.1.pdf) defines precision, recall and F1 against a reference alignment. [OAEI 2026 tracks](https://oaei.ontologymatching.org/2026/) were checked for a relevant standard benchmark. No EIA-to-this-locked-vocabulary reference alignment was found in those tracks or the targeted search. OAEI is a standard benchmark family, but its unrelated-domain datasets cannot directly score this route-specific correspondence table. No OAEI score has been measured or claimed. Catalogue completeness and availability are separate from semantic mapping accuracy; do not report a precision/recall/F1 without an independently established reference and a stated denominator. The owner's frozen in-domain checks remain required and are not an OAEI benchmark.
- **Dialogue-state tracking, for the later implementation phase:** [the owner-cited DST survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.14627) and [original MultiWOZ 2.1 paper, 2020](https://aclanthology.org/2020.lrec-1.53/) identify accumulated slot state and joint state accuracy as established practice and evaluation. MultiWOZ is an established benchmark; its travel/service slots do not match this locked EIA form. It has not been run here, and EIA case scores must not be presented as MultiWOZ results. The owner's separate start/end-slot decision is a scoped application, not a new dialogue architecture. Any additional benchmark adapter or paid run needs its scope and cost reconciled before execution.

Searches included UNSD correspondence-table guidance, W3C mapping relations, OAEI reference-alignment evaluation and current tracks, EIA energy-series mapping benchmarks, EIA MSN documentation, and the original MultiWOZ paper. The search found established methods and metrics but no directly applicable public EIA correspondence benchmark. That is a bounded search finding, not proof that no such dataset exists. The current classification files remain drafts; no coverage grid, scored set or implementation is being declared complete by this research update.
