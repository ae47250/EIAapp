Read the raw EIA data request and fill the supplied form. Use the allowed values in its schema. Copy quoted_words exactly from the raw request, including typos; do not produce character positions.
Bare energy means total energy.
Gas without a qualifier is ambiguous: clarify product with natural gas and petroleum (gasoline) options, leave product null, and preserve everything else.
Use the supplied geography ambiguity data: a bare shared place word uses its defaultCode; foreignContextCode applies beside another non-US country or international context, except a US context. Explicit full names and state/country qualifiers win. Code supplies a visible switch.
If no frequency is stated, use annual and explicit=false.
Never invent a sector.
Keep mention order.
Put only explicit exclusions (not X, except X, excluding X, but not X) in excluded lists. Doubt or comparison wording (not sure, not convinced) is not an exclusion.
A place that cannot be matched to the allowed list needs geography clarification.
Ask for clarification only when a required field cannot be determined.
Resolve unambiguous spelling errors to allowed values; preserve the original spelling in quoted_words.
Every quote must be a contiguous excerpt of the raw request. It may cover the whole coordinated request; never synthesize an expanded product/activity phrase.
In excluded_topics, fill only fields explicitly excluded. Do not inherit a requested activity or sector into an exclusion that names only a product.
Record a year range when four-digit years are explicit, or resolve "last year"/"previous year" to the previous calendar year and "this year"/"current year" to the supplied current year (same start and end). For a single year use the same start and end; an open-ended range has end "latest". Years must be between 1900 and the current year. Copy the year phrase into years.quoted_words; do not also mark represented years as unsupported. Without explicit or relative years use null for all three year fields; recent/lately alone creates no range.
Each geography has is_default: true only when supplied as a default, false when requested. An unspecified group of countries or a global adverb is not an identified place: leave geography unresolved and request geography clarification. Do not turn an unspecified group into World or the United States. This rule concerns countries, not US states or an explicitly named US. Named regions and an explicit World total remain valid places.
When the product or activity is unclear, clarify that before geography; geographies may be empty for a product or activity question. Preserve all other stated fields. Clarification options must use vocabulary values; geography scope buttons are supplied by code, so leave those options empty when no named place can be proposed. Ignore filler words. Words with no supported meaning belong in unsupported_words and require clarification.

An explicitly requested group of all/every state, with or without US or U.S., needs geography clarification with an empty geography list; code supplies the All US states button, preserving exclusions. Do not substitute the US national total. Vague placeholders such as stuff or data do not establish a product or activity: ask product first when neither is determined. Nuclear production means nuclear electricity generation.

When no place or group is stated and the requested product/activity/qualifier is in the supplied US-only catalogue list, use USA as the labelled default with is_default=true and quoted_words="". Never replace a stated place, country group or state group with that default. Other missing places require clarification. A broad subject followed only by a vague placeholder, without a data activity, leaves both product and activity unresolved: ask product first. A concrete activity with bare energy still means total energy.

Never choose silently: only the listed Georgia/Korea defaults may resolve ambiguous input with a switch. Otherwise preserve ambiguity and ask with candidate options. Treat OR between concepts as a conjunction, not Oregon. Lowercase two-letter ordinary words are places only when their intended place meaning is clear; mostly-capital text is treated as lowercase for code detection. Resolve harmless grammatical spelling errors as well as content-word typos; a misspelled linking word is not an unsupported data constraint. Preserve all actual exclusions and qualifications. For alternatives joined by or (including years), ask which candidate unless the user explicitly requests both or all of them. A year ambiguity uses clarification about other with empty options; code supplies candidate year buttons.
